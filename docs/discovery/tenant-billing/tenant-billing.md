## 1. Problem statement

Ikaro is a multi-tenant SaaS, and today nothing in it models Ikaro itself being paid. There is no plan, price, subscription, entitlement or tenant billing status anywhere in the code (verified: no Stripe/payment code; `platform.tenants` has only `is_active`, which nothing enforces on the auth path). Tenants are provisioned by an operator script (UC-024) and every feature is available to every tenant.

The business needs to:

1. Charge each tenant a **price per feature, set by its country** (BR → BRL, US → USD), with one list for everyone and no per-tenant negotiation.
2. **Sell features separately** (hotsite only, or hotsite + booking + loyalty + chatbot + lead form; recurring reservations and availability alerts are part of booking, decision 80) and switch each one on only while it is paid for.
3. **Stop tenants that do not pay** without a hard "login blocked" wall: a lapsed tenant's manager can still log in, but only to a billing page where they can pay.
4. Let **anyone start a 14-day trial on their own** (self-signup from Ikaro's own hotsite), with all features, no card.
5. Keep Ikaro simple: **the payment provider owns everything money-related it can**.

## 2. Standing rules (the decisions that govern everything else)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | **The payment provider owns the money.** Charging, retries, hosted payment pages, receipts, refunds, chargebacks, saved cards and Pix mandates are the provider's. Ikaro stores only the status it needs (see R8). Ikaro never stores card data and never renders card forms. |
| R2 | **Entitlement gates, preference tunes, and both must pass.** An *entitlement* (Ikaro grants the feature because the tenant pays for it) is a different layer from a manager *preference* (hotsite `enabled`, `settings.*`). Effective availability = entitled AND enabled. Losing an entitlement never deletes or rewrites the manager's preference; regaining it resumes exactly as left. |
| R3 | **Everything sellable is a feature**, including the hotsite. A plan is a bundle of features. There is no always-included base. |
| R4 | **Price is per feature × country × tier × cycle**, from one list, in the country's currency. No FX conversion and **no per-tenant prices or negotiation**. BR prices are tax-inclusive; US prices are net and receive a separately displayed Stripe Tax amount once the customer's location is known. When an invoice is issued, it selects the list-price version in force at that invoice's **service-period start** and snapshots both its feature lines and any tax result; no issued invoice is edited (the only writes after insert are status/payment fields and, in the later US slice, the provider-finalized tax/amount of a US charge). A recovery charge is the one documented exception to the period-start rule: it snapshots the versions in force on its issue date (§4.3). A list change is **operator-only, dated and announced**. An increase needs at least `PRICE_CHANGE_MIN_NOTICE_DAYS + CHARGE_ISSUE_LEAD_DAYS` notice (30 + 10 days by default), so managers know the amount before the early invoice is issued. Annual / semester contracts are extra price rows (`cycle`), deferred. |
| R5 | **Nothing fiscal is required to *use* the product; the minimum billing identity is required to *charge* it, and formats follow the billing country.** Address and tax-ID fields, labels and validation come from the existing per-country `CountrySpec` (`AddressSpec` + a new tax-ID entry), in the tenant's language. The minimum is what the provider needs to create a payable charge: **BR = CPF or CNPJ** (Asaas requires `cpfCnpj` to create a customer), collected at signup; **US = the full address** (street, city, state, ZIP — Stripe Tax needs at least a 5-digit ZIP and discourages ZIP-only), collected before the first paid-plan selection. Legal name and billing email stay optional. Nota fiscal (NFS-e) is a fiscal process outside Ikaro v1 (handled in the provider console / by the accountant). |
| R6 | **Access control reads state, not events.** The tenant's billing status and entitled features are read synchronously through one shared `TenantAccessPort`. A transient lookup failure fails open so Billing downtime cannot lock out a paying tenant; a successfully-read **missing subscription** is a provisioning/data-integrity incident, never unrestricted access — except for a tenant younger than `PROVISIONING_GRACE_MINUTES` (default 15), whose subscription is still being created by the asynchronous `TenantProvisioned` handler: that window is treated as a pending trial (allowed, no alert), and a self-heal job creates the missing trial if the window expires (decision 108). Events trigger emails and durable cross-context work only. |
| R7 | **Billing never mutates the manager's `isPublished`.** Effective published = manager's `isPublished` AND tenant operational. |
| R8 | **Ikaro owns three policies only:** the access ladder, the entitlement set, and the clean-up. Everything else is mirrored from the provider via webhook + a reconcile job. |
| R9 | **Providers behind a port.** `BillingProviderPort` is defined in Ikaro's terms (start payment, give a payment link, cancel, enable auto-billing; inbound normalized events), never in the provider's. Both Asaas (BRL) and Stripe (USD) are designed in the billing scope; a country/provider activation is released only after its real account and sandbox flow is verified. |
| R10 | **Billing emails to the manager are exempt from the suspension gate**; every tenant-facing and customer-facing send stops while suspended. |
| R11 | **Plan changes are self-service.** The manager adds or removes features and picks the chatbot tier on a "Plano" tab, sees the new total before saving, and no contact is needed. There is no operator step for prices. |
| R13 | **Operator-free by default.** Every situation is owned, in order, by the provider (money mechanics), the system (jobs, webhooks, self-healing), the tenant (self-service) and only then the operator. Operator work is limited to the edge cases mapped in `tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md` §4; humans are alerted by exceptions only, never handed a daily list; the KPI is operator interventions per 100 active tenants per month (decisions 106–111). |
| R12 | **`exempt` is the one switch for "never charge this tenant".** An exempt subscription has every feature, is never charged, never suspended, never purged and receives no billing emails. It is set by an audited operator endpoint (or at provisioning) and can be cleared again; Ikaro's own tenant can never be un-exempted. It is not a price of zero and not the global kill switch. |

## 3. Industry research and provider decision record

**How the market structures this** (vendor docs; blog numbers are directional):
- Four layers: catalog → subscription → entitlements → dunning/access. Stripe recommends persisting entitlements in your own DB from its webhook instead of calling the API per request ([Stripe Entitlements](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/entitlements)).
- Subscription statuses `trialing → active → past_due → unpaid/canceled`; failed payments move to `past_due` and the provider retries ([Stripe subscriptions](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/overview.md)). Typical grace is 7–14 days with retries around days 1, 3, 5, 7; cutting access immediately raises churn ([SaaS billing guide](https://fungies.io/subscription-billing-implementation-saas-guide)).
- Access is graduated (banner → restricted → suspended) and the customer does not choose the due date: it anchors to the contract date. Updating the payment method and downloading invoices is done in the provider's hosted portal.
- Sales-led SaaS invoices manually; product-led SaaS uses processor subscriptions; most B2B SaaS ends up hybrid with its own subscription table and the processor as one way to collect. Ikaro is designed for both.

**Provider decision (2026-10-26): Asaas (BRL) and Stripe (USD), behind one port.**

| Provider | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Asaas** | First adapter (BR) | Recurring Pix (Pix Automático) supported, and since a May 2026 changelog Asaas can generate later charges itself ([Pix Automático](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/pix-automatico)); subscriptions create charges ahead of due date (default 40 days, configurable 14 or 7) ([Assinaturas](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/assinaturas)); webhook auth by `asaas-access-token` header, at-least-once delivery, dedupe on event `id`, queue pauses after 15 consecutive failures, events kept 14 days ([webhooks](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/receba-eventos-do-asaas-no-seu-endpoint-de-webhook)); NFS-e with the charge, PJ account required, about R$ 0,49 per note ([FAQ](https://docs.asaas.com/docs/faq-de-notas-fiscais)). |
| **Stripe** | USD adapter, **in scope of the same milestone sequence** (US signup is enabled only after its real account/sandbox verification) | Best API/portal/hosted invoices. Verified 2026-10-27 (`tenant-billing_PROVIDERS.md` §2): a Brazilian CNPJ account is allowed but settles in BRL only, with no USD payouts and no ACH; first US account = that account, cards only (decision 60). Hosted object = **Invoices** with `automatic_tax`; Stripe Tax calculates only where the account is registered and never files. Ikaro's selling company remains merchant of record and responsible for registration/filing. Real-account checks are launch gates. |
| **Appmax** | Rejected for this use | E-commerce checkout; recurring product exists but webhooks, NFS-e and Pix Automático are not confirmed ([appmax.com.br](https://appmax.com.br/)). |

## 4. Domain model summary

### 4.1 Features and dependencies

`FeatureKey` (closed enum in `@ikaro/types`, mirrored per the `closed-enum-registry` detector): `HOTSITE`, `BOOKING`, `LOYALTY`, `CHATBOT`, `LEAD_FORM`. Recurring reservations and availability alerts are **part of `BOOKING`** (decision 80), not separate features.

| Feature | Requires |
|---|---|
| `BOOKING` | `HOTSITE` (assumed: customers reach booking through the hotsite) |
| `LOYALTY` | `BOOKING` |
| `CHATBOT`, `LEAD_FORM` | `HOTSITE` |

`HOTSITE` is therefore the root of every other feature and is effectively mandatory: it can never be removed (the plan keeps at least one feature, and every other feature requires it). There is no billable booking-only tenant.

Adding a feature whose dependency is not active is rejected; removing a feature that another active feature depends on is rejected. Removing `BOOKING` is also rejected while any `PENDING`, `INFO_REQUESTED` or `APPROVED` booking, recurring schedule or availability alert extends on or after the removal's effective date (`BILLING_FEATURE_IN_USE`, decision 81); once a removal is saved, the bookable window is capped at that date (reusing the booking-window concept of M23, verified in story discovery), so no new commitment can appear after the check.

**Tiers.** `CHATBOT` has tiers (`BASIC`, `PLUS`; the display names Básico / Plus are final, decision 76). Billing only stores the tier on the item; the chatbot code (Platform) maps tier → LLM model + caps. Trial tenants get `BASIC`.

### 4.2 Aggregates (context `Billing`, a 7th bounded context)

| Aggregate | Owns |
|---|---|
| `Subscription` (1 per tenant) | status, suspension reason, currency, country, **timezone (snapshot)**, cycle (`MONTHLY` only in v1), anchor day, trial end, period, `pastDueSince`, `graceUntil`, `cancelAt`, `exempt`, provider ids; child **items** (feature, tier, `startsAt`, `endsAt` — **no price**) |
| `BillingAccount` (1 per tenant) | tax identity (type, number: **BR required from signup, US optional**), legal name (optional), billing email (optional), address, provider customer id, accepted terms |
| `Charge` | one bill for one period (due date, amount, status, method, provider charge id, payment link) + **lines** (price snapshot per feature) |
| `FeaturePrice` (platform-wide, no `tenant_id`) | catalog identity + dated versions per feature × country × tier × cycle; items reference the tuple logically and charge lines snapshot the selected version |

Entitlement is not an aggregate and not a stored fact: `entitled = SUSPENDED ? none : TRIALING ? every sellable feature (chatbot BASIC) : the subscription items active now` (decision 83). Items hold only the *selected plan* (what is paid for, from which period); there are no trial rows and suspension does not end-date them. Free access (Ikaro's own tenant and tenants that must never be charged; the existing staging tenants are backfilled `exempt` once, while new test tenants are charged normally against the fake or sandbox, §5.3) is the `exempt` flag on the subscription, not a price of zero. Items carry no price: the amount of a charge is read from the price list when the charge is issued.

Boundary note: paying a `Charge` may move the `Subscription` back to `ACTIVE`. This is two aggregates in one use case, in one transaction, with a row lock on the subscription (`findByIdForUpdate`). Status is recomputable (the reconcile job re-derives it), so the invariant does not depend on the transaction alone.

### 4.3 Status ladder

`TRIALING → ACTIVE → PAST_DUE (14 days) → SUSPENDED`; a trial whose first invoice is unpaid goes **directly** `TRIALING → SUSPENDED` (no grace).

| Status | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `TRIALING` | Everything, all features, tier `BASIC` for chatbot | 14 days, ending at the tenant-local midnight that closes day 14 (decision 84); no card. The Plan tab chooses the first paid period but never changes trial entitlements. **Default first paid selection = every trial feature continues** (chatbot `BASIC`) unless the manager changes it. The first invoice is issued 10 days before trial end, is due at trial end, and—when paid—anchors later renewals to the trial-end day. **If the trial ends without a paid first invoice — unpaid at its due date, or no charge could be issued — the subscription becomes `SUSPENDED` (`NON_PAYMENT`) at trial end. There is no `PAST_DUE` grace after a trial.** The sweep waits `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_HOURS` (default 24) after trial end and re-reads the charge from the provider before suspending, so a boleto or Pix settled at the deadline is not punished (decision 86). |
| `ACTIVE` | Per active items | |
| `PAST_DUE` | **Full access**, banner for managers | 14 days (`graceUntil` can extend it by hand). Provider retries; Ikaro sends dunning reminders (CAND-34). |
| `SUSPENDED` (reason `NON_PAYMENT`, `CANCELED`, `PAYMENT_REVERSED` or `OPERATOR`) | See 4.4 | No expiry until the clean-up (4.6). **No new charges are issued while suspended.** A full confirmed refund or completed chargeback reversal that leaves the current period unpaid suspends immediately as `PAYMENT_REVERSED`; a dispute merely opened does not. `OPERATOR` is an abuse/takedown suspension set by the operator: no recovery charge is issued, payment does not lift it, and only the audited `unsuspend` (valid for this reason alone) does (decision 87). While the provider is unhealthy the sweep applies a **suspension freeze** (decision 108). |

Reactivation: the old open charge is **never edited or re-dated**. **When the sweep suspends a subscription for `NON_PAYMENT` or `PAYMENT_REVERSED`**, Billing cancels the stale open charge (locally and at the provider) and immediately issues one **recovery charge** (so "Pagar agora" on the suspended page is always the recovery charge); for a `CANCELED` suspension it is issued when the manager clicks "Reativar assinatura". If the provider refuses to cancel the stale charge because its payment was already received, the stale charge is recorded `PAID` and reactivates like any payment (the recovery charge is then cancelled); if both are ever paid, the second is refunded in the provider console and recorded `REFUNDED` (decision 85). The recovery charge is due `RECOVERY_CHARGE_DUE_DAYS` (default 7) after issue and is: a one-month charge, amount fixed at issue from the price versions in force on the issue date, lines = the current items. When that charge is confirmed paid, the subscription becomes `ACTIVE`, `current_period_start` = the payment moment, `anchor_day` = the payment's tenant-local day clamped to 28, ; the period lives on the subscription only — the recovery charge keeps `period_start`/`period_end` NULL and `paid_at` is its start (decision 82), so after issue only status and payment fields are ever written to a charge. The tenant never owes the months it could not use. (The 14 grace days before suspension were not paid for — accepted cost of the grace period.) **Constants** defined once here: `CHARGE_ISSUE_LEAD_DAYS = 10`, `PRICE_CHANGE_MIN_NOTICE_DAYS = 30`, `RECOVERY_CHARGE_DUE_DAYS = 7`, grace = 14 days, `PROVISIONING_GRACE_MINUTES = 15`, `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_HOURS = 24`.

**Trial → paid:** a `TRIALING` subscription whose first charge is paid stays `TRIALING` until `trialEndsAt`; the sweep then turns it `ACTIVE` (period starts at trial end, `anchor_day` = trial-end local day clamped to 28, `has_ever_paid` already true from the payment). If the first charge is unpaid at trial end, the subscription is suspended (decision 42).

### 4.4 What suspension does

| Who / what | While `SUSPENDED` |
|---|---|
| Manager | Can log in; reaches **only** the billing page (everything else redirects there). |
| Staff | Can log in; sees only "Esta conta está suspensa. Fale com o gerente." |
| Customer | Can log in to their account; sees bookings with an "Empresa inativa" badge; history is viewable; open-booking details, rescheduling and new booking are blocked, but a customer may **cancel** an existing future booking. Loyalty balance/history stay read-only; earning and redemption are blocked. |
| Tenant selection (staff with several tenants) | A suspended tenant **stays listed**, marked "Suspensa"; selecting it logs in and staff see the suspended notice. Never hidden, never an error page. (Customers sign in per tenant slug; no customer selector exists today — a future switcher must follow the same rule.) |
| Public hotsite | Effective-unpublished. Shows "Página não publicada" (reason-neutral), keeping the top bar with team login and customer login. The same page, with the same bar, is shown for **every** unpublished hotsite (a different page for suspended tenants would leak the reason). `Unavailable` does not render the bar today — a code change (decision 49). |
| Notifications / reminders / loyalty emails | Stop for that tenant (gate at the notification send). **Billing emails to the manager still go out (R10).** Events produced meanwhile are dropped, not replayed on reactivation. |
| Crons | Data-maintenance crons (loyalty expiry, retention purges) run for suspended tenants too, through the shared tenant job helper's explicit `includeSuspended` option (decision 88); feature-delivery crons (reminders, renewal reminders, alert matchers) use the default helper, which excludes suspended and unentitled tenants. Loyalty points expire on schedule even during a suspension (deliberate: gives tenants a reason to pay on time); their *emails* are stopped by the notification gate. |
| Bookings already confirmed | Remain in the tenant's data; not visible to staff; customers see the badge. |

### 4.5 Cancelling and re-enabling

The manager can cancel on the billing page. An `ACTIVE` subscription cancels at the **end of its last paid period** (`cancelAt` = end of the latest `PAID` period, so an already-paid next period is honoured; access continues until then; banner "Cancelada — acesso até dd/mm"). On confirmation, every unpaid charge whose service period begins on/after `cancelAt` is immediately cancelled locally and at the provider, so no cancelled service remains payable. A `PAST_DUE` subscription cancels immediately because it has no paid period left: it becomes `SUSPENDED` with reason `CANCELED`. A `TRIALING` one whose first invoice is already `PAID` cancels at trial end (`cancelAt = trialEndsAt`, access continues; decision 89); otherwise it cancels immediately. "Reativar assinatura" clears a still-future `cancelAt`; after suspension it issues one new recovery charge for one month (see §4.3), and confirmed payment starts a new period anchored to the payment date. Suspension does not end-date items; the recovery charge's lines are the current items. Nothing is refunded.

### 4.6 Clean-up (tenant erasure)

A daily job selects tenants that stay `SUSPENDED` too long: **90 days** if the subscription never paid (`has_ever_paid = false`), **180 days** if it paid at least once — regardless of the suspension reason. Warning emails go to the manager **30 and 7 days** before the deadline and say what will be deleted and how to ask for an export (self-service export is a later story, G11). Selection creates a durable `TenantPurgeJob`; it then publishes `TenantPurgeRequested`. Each bounded context deletes its own data idempotently and publishes its completion after commit; Billing records the completions. Storage cleanup, provider cancellation and Billing's anonymized fiscal history are also required steps. **What Billing keeps** (final retention period: the accountant, §11): the `subscriptions` row without personal data, and every `charges` / `charge_lines` / `charge_tax_lines` row (amounts, dates, statuses, with `tenant_id` as an opaque id and the composite FKs intact). **What Billing deletes:** the `billing_accounts` personal data (tax ID, legal name, billing email, address, `terms_accepted_by`, provider customer id), the `signup_registrations` row, `provider_events` and the payload of its `operator_audit` rows. Only after every required step completes does Platform delete the tenant root and release its slug. The job is an operational process (`REQUESTED`/`IN_PROGRESS`/`FAILED`/`COMPLETED`), not a Subscription status. Tenant erasure does not exist anywhere in the code today: every bounded context, storage, chatbot and audit data each need their own deletion story, sequenced before the purge cron is enabled. After a completed purge the signup trial rule of §6 is evaluated again.

### 4.7 Pricing

`FeaturePrice`: `(feature_key, country_code, tier, cycle)` → `amount_minor` + `currency`. Countries in v1: **BR (BRL), US (USD)**. No fallback row. Adding a country = a country spec + price rows. **BR list = the approved launch catalog (decision 77); US list = staging-only until the Stripe checks (decision 78).** Ikaro absorbs provider fees:

| Feature | BR | US |
|---|---|---|
| HOTSITE | R$ 49,00 | US$ 12 |
| BOOKING (includes recurring reservations and availability alerts) | R$ 109,00 | US$ 26 |
| LOYALTY | R$ 29,00 | US$ 7 |
| CHATBOT BASIC / PLUS | R$ 39,00 / R$ 79,00 | US$ 9 / US$ 19 |
| LEAD_FORM | R$ 19,00 | US$ 5 |

**Self-service plan changes (R11):** additions, removals, chatbot upgrades and downgrades all become effective at the **next paid service-period start**. They never change current-period entitlements, require no proration and cannot be used without payment. During the **trial**, all features remain enabled; the Plan tab only records the first paid selection. The proposed final selection is validated as one dependency graph, so dependent features may be added together in one save. A plan always keeps at least one feature. `PAST_DUE` and `SUSPENDED` plans are read-only. If the next invoice is already issued and unpaid, it is cancelled and re-issued from the scheduled next-period selection (a new `PERIOD` charge for the same period; the cancelled one stays as history and the uniqueness index ignores cancelled charges); a paid invoice is never changed, and if the provider refuses the cancel because it was just paid, the change waits for the following renewal. **List price changes:** the operator schedules a dated version and announces it by email to every affected `TRIALING`, `ACTIVE` or `PAST_DUE` manager (trial tenants included, so the first early invoice never surprises them). `effective_from` is a **service-period start date**, and an increase requires at least `PRICE_CHANGE_MIN_NOTICE_DAYS + CHARGE_ISSUE_LEAD_DAYS` notice (default 40 days). A catalog row may be hidden from new selection while existing subscribers continue renewing it; a planned retirement needs a separately announced migration date. Annual billing is deferred. No coupons. Billing country is fixed when the subscription starts. **A newly selected feature starts only at the next paid period, up to about a month later:** the locked-feature screens and the Plano tab say so ("começa em dd/mm"), they never imply instant access. This delay is the main upsell friction, so add-feature conversion is reviewed after launch (decision 90). **US totals shown on the Summary and Plano tabs are net of tax** and carry a "+ imposto calculado na cobrança" label; the tax amount exists only on the issued charge (R4). A suspended tenant is not notified of price changes; its recovery charge snapshots the price in force on its issue date.

## 5. Architecture

- **One `Billing` context** owns catalog, subscription, charges, the provider adapter, the webhook, crons and the access resolver. **No projection in Platform.**
- **`TenantAccessPort`** lives in `src/shared/ports/` (cross-cutting, like the tenant-settings port the request filter already uses). It returns status, suspension reason and entitled features. Billing implements it with a short-TTL cache (`CachingXxx` pattern, `CachePort`), invalidated best-effort after commit. Callers pass an explicit `tenantId`. A temporary lookup error fails open; a successful read with no subscription is a provisioning/data-integrity incident (after the `PROVISIONING_GRACE_MINUTES` window, R6), emits an alert only if self-heal fails, and never grants unrestricted access.
- **Enforcement points** (CAND-15..20): BFF login routing and a global guard (Guard, never an Interceptor), web `proxy.ts` + dashboard layouts, the hotsite manifest/booking page/sitemap/robots (effective published), the notification send gate, each feature's use cases (entitlement), the public chatbot endpoint, locked UI in the dashboard.
- **Access cache (decision 91):** TTLs are short and asymmetric. An operational answer is cached 60 s in the shared port and 30 s in the BFF guard; a `SUSPENDED` answer only 10 s in both, because a paying tenant must come back fast and suspended tenants are rare. `SubscriptionSuspended`/`SubscriptionReactivated` also trigger an explicit best-effort invalidation on every instance; the TTL is the backstop. The billing page and the access calls it makes bypass the BFF cache. All values are env-configurable.
- **Enforcement without scattered flags:** see §5.1.
- **Events:** `ChargeIssued`, `ChargePaid`, `SubscriptionPastDue`, `SubscriptionSuspended`, `SubscriptionReactivated`, `SubscriptionCancelled`, `PlanChanged`, `BillingReminderDue` (`TRIAL_ENDING` | `CHARGE_DUE_SOON` | `CHARGE_OVERDUE`), `SubscriptionPriceChangeAnnounced` (one per affected tenant), and purge command/completion events (decision 102). Ikaro sends all of these emails itself (decision 59). Each event needs a Billing audit-log subscription.
- **Webhook:** a `@Public()` BFF route validates `asaas-access-token`, preserves the raw body and forwards internally. The backend resolves the tenant, deduplicates the provider event, applies the local charge/subscription state and writes any outbox event **in one transaction**, then returns exactly `200` (Asaas treats 201/204/3xx as failures, decision 61). Email delivery is asynchronous from the outbox. Unknown customers are acknowledged and logged; provider I/O never occurs inside `txManager.run()`.
- **Crons follow the existing trigger pattern, not a bespoke HTTP one:** Cloud Scheduler publishes a tenant-less trigger over Pub/Sub, delivered through the shared `/pubsub/push` receiver to a handler registered with `ITriggerBus.registerTrigger(name, handler, consumer)`; the `/cron/billing-*` controller endpoints are only the local/manual publisher behind `InternalApiGuard`, exactly like `CronLeadFormController`. Jobs: issue charges (daily), status sweep (**hourly**, decision 92: trial end, due-date overdue, `PAST_DUE` → `SUSPENDED`, cancel-at, honouring the suspension freeze), billing reminders (daily, CAND-34), reconcile with the provider (daily for every open charge, hourly for open charges of at-risk tenants, plus an on-demand single-charge refresh when a manager returns from the hosted page; webhook queues can stall and events expire after 14 days), self-heal and provider health (CAND-35), provider-events retention purge, tenant clean-up (no daily ops digest, decision 107). Each job selects its own tenants through standalone indexes (no tenant id arrives with the trigger).
### 5.1 Enforcement without scattered flags

A feature or the suspension state is checked at a few **declarative choke points**, never with ad-hoc `if (tenant.hasChatbot)` code inside use cases.

1. **HTTP (backend and BFF):** `@RequiresFeature(FeatureKey.X)` declares entitlement and `@SuspensionAccess(...)` declares the exceptional suspended-state route policy (`MANAGER_BILLING`, `STAFF_NOTICE`, `CUSTOMER_HISTORY`, `AUTH_TENANT_SWITCH`, `PUBLIC_NONE`, or `DEFAULT_DENY`). `@NoFeatureGate()` is only for health, auth and webhooks. One global **`TenantAccessGuard`** reads metadata with `Reflector`, asks `TenantAccessPort`, and throws typed errors. Record-dependent exceptions—customer cancellation is allowed but an open-booking detail is not—remain Booking domain policy, not a guessed route rule.
2. **Completeness is CI-enforced:** an `architecture-check` detector requires explicit entitlement and suspension metadata (or `@NoFeatureGate`) on every tenant-scoped route. Rollout (decision 94): the detector starts in warn mode while the annotation story is in flight, because it would otherwise fail every open branch, and becomes enforcing in the PR that finishes the annotation; a route without metadata defaults to entitlement-allowed and suspension-denied.
3. **Events (opt-in gate):** `IEventBus.subscribe` has no options parameter today, so this adds an optional fourth argument. A handler that sends something outbound (notifications, loyalty emails, reminders) declares it — `subscribe(Event.name, handler, CONSUMER, { requires: FeatureKey.LOYALTY, skipWhenSuspended: true })` — and the dispatcher then skips and logs it for suspended or unentitled tenants. **The default is to run:** audit-log handlers, state-sync handlers and Billing's own handlers are never skipped, so a suspension cannot create audit gaps or half-applied state. A detector requires every handler that sends something outbound to declare `skipWhenSuspended` explicitly (true or false), so a forgotten flag fails CI (decision 93). Skipped events are dropped, not replayed. Handlers contain no access logic.
4. **Crons:** one shared tenant-scoped job helper iterates tenants through a single method that already excludes suspended and unentitled tenants (one batch query); it takes an explicit `includeSuspended` option for data-maintenance crons (decision 88); individual crons do not check. Billing's own jobs deliberately do not use it.
5. **Notifications:** the single send path (CAND-18), with Billing templates exempt.
6. **Web:** one route-to-feature registry feeds the dashboard nav (locked items), the layouts and `proxy.ts`, plus one `<FeatureGate feature>` wrapper for the locked state.
7. **Kill switch:** `FEATURE_FLAG_BILLING_ENFORCEMENT` (default `true`; `false` = fail open everywhere). It is read in exactly one place, so every choke point honors it; it is an emergency switch, not normal policy.

Adding a sellable feature then means: add the `FeatureKey`, decorate its controllers, tag its subscriptions. Nothing else. Risks: the dispatcher change touches the shared event bus and needs care; the `@Public()` slug resolution adds a tenant lookup on those routes (cached).

### 5.2 Changes to existing Platform provisioning

Verified against the code: `ProvisionTenantUseCase` takes `{name, slug, adminEmail, country_code, timezone?}` and `TenantProvisioned` carries only `{name, slug, adminEmail, timezone}`. Billing and signup need more, so promotion includes:
- `TenantProvisioned` gains `countryCode` and an optional billing hint — `exempt`, optional feature subset, and, for self-signup, `googleSub`, `terms { version, acceptedAt, acceptedBy }` and the BR `taxId { type, number }` — bump `eventVersion`; the Billing handler creates the subscription, items, `BillingAccount` (with tax ID and terms when present) and the `signup_registrations` row in one transaction. A non-exempt BR tenant without a valid `taxId` is rejected by `POST /internal/tenants` and the script (same rule as signup, decision 72). Billing's trial handler reads `countryCode`, `timezone` and the hint from the event and never calls Platform.
- `ProvisionTenantUseCase`/`POST /internal/tenants`/`scripts/provision-tenant.sh` gain an optional `exempt` flag. No `language` parameter is added: **language always derives from country** (BR → `pt-BR`, US → `en`), as today.
- Self-signup records the creator's Google `sub` and the accepted Terms version/time in the new `billing.signup_registrations` table (data model) and hands the terms to Billing through the same event hint.
- **A non-exempt US tenant is rejected while `FEATURE_FLAG_SIGNUP_US` is off**, by the script path as well as by self-signup (Billing fails loudly and alerts); exempt US tenants (test tenants) are allowed.

### 5.3 Non-production testing (nothing charges anyone, every flow is testable)

`exempt` is **not** the testing mechanism: it switches charging off. In non-production **every tenant is charged normally against a mock or sandbox gateway**, and provider choice is per **environment**, never per tenant. Facts verified in the repo: `APP_ENV` is `local | staging | production`; `EMAIL_ADAPTER=mailhog` is already forbidden outside `local`; there is a Pub/Sub emulator and a BFF dev-login; no clock abstraction exists today.

| Environment (`APP_ENV`) | Provider (`merchant_profile_key` given to new subscriptions) | Email | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| `local` | `BRL_FAKE` / `USD_FAKE` — `FakeBillingProvider`; the real adapters can be opted into with a tunnel for webhooks | Mailhog | injectable clock, "advance N days" |
| CI per PR | the fake | Mailhog | fixed clock |
| CI nightly (scheduled job) | n/a — shared contract tests run against the Asaas sandbox and Stripe test mode to catch drift between the fake and the real APIs | — | — |
| `staging` | `BRL_ASAAS_SANDBOX`, `USD_STRIPE_TEST` | Brevo with a **recipient allowlist**; anything else is dropped and logged `SKIPPED_NOT_ALLOWLISTED` | operator "advance time" action |
| `production` | live profile keys only | Brevo | real clock |

- **Fake provider** (`FakeBillingProvider`, an `InMemoryXxx`-style double that is also wired as a real adapter in `local`): implements the whole `BillingProviderPort`, returns a hosted-link URL served by the app, and can emit **signed or tokened webhooks to the app's own endpoint** on demand: paid per method with the verified Asaas event sequences (Pix `RECEIVED` only; boleto `CONFIRMED`→`RECEIVED`; card `CONFIRMED`, `RECEIVED` later), overdue, refund, chargeback, **duplicate, delayed and out-of-order** delivery, provider 5xx, expired link, invalid location. A forgiving fake would hide the money loop's real bugs, so it reproduces the verified behaviour.
- **One contract suite, two implementations:** the same tests run against the fake (every PR) and the real sandbox/test mode (nightly). Passing both is the proof the swap is safe.
- **Safeguards:** a live adapter refuses to start unless `APP_ENV=production` (validated like the existing mailhog rule in `env.validation.ts`); a `*_FAKE` or `*_SANDBOX` profile key is rejected in production; the simulate and clock endpoints exist only when `APP_ENV` is not `production`, behind `InternalApiGuard` + `PlatformAdminGuard`.
- **Clock:** a new `IClock` port (system clock in production) used by every Billing date decision (trial end, due dates, grace, cancel-at, purge age) and by the sweep jobs; non-production gets an operator-only `POST /internal/dev/clock/advance` and a script like `provision-tenant.sh`. No UI.
- **Seed script:** creates one tenant per state — `TRIALING`, `ACTIVE`, `ACTIVE` with a scheduled cancel, `PAST_DUE`, `SUSPENDED` (`NON_PAYMENT` and `CANCELED`), `exempt`, US tenant with and without address — so every screen and job can be exercised.
- **Production smoke test:** live mode cannot be sandboxed; a smoke test is one real small charge on a **dedicated non-exempt production test tenant** (never Ikaro's own tenant, which is always exempt, R12), then a refund (decision 95).
- **Notification change:** the existing send path gains `EMAIL_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST` (required when `APP_ENV` is `staging`, ignored in `production` and `local`) and the `NotificationStatus` values `SKIPPED_TENANT_SUSPENDED` and `SKIPPED_NOT_ALLOWLISTED` (today it is `PENDING | SENT | FAILED`, so the type and any DB check change together).

### 5.4 Anti-pattern check and cost tail

- **Anti-pattern check (CLAUDE.md §8):** `useClass` not `useExisting`; no new cross-context Port+Adapter (one shared port); money and tax-id value objects with typed errors (`vo-bare-error`); new error codes in both locale files; check `@ikaro/types` before adding web types; validation schemas duplicated in BFF and backend; no raw SQL outside repositories; `@Global()` modules must `export` their tokens; architecture-policy entries for any permitted edge.
- **Cost / infra tail:** new third-party dependencies (Asaas, Stripe), secrets (API keys and webhook tokens, sandbox and live separately, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), 7 Cloud Scheduler jobs (issue, sweep, reminders, reconcile, self-heal, provider-events purge, tenant purge), a nightly sandbox contract-test CI job, the staging `EMAIL_RECIPIENT_ALLOWLIST` env, a new schema `billing`, the 7th context in `BOUNDED_CONTEXTS.md`, platform-level billing email templates (pt-BR and en; not copied per tenant, decision 100), Terraform for scheduler/secrets.

## 6. Self-signup, trial and Ikaro's own tenant

- Ikaro is a tenant too (its hotsite is the marketing site). That tenant is **exempt from billing** (`exempt = true`: all features, never charged, never suspended, never purged).
- Hotsite module **`TRIAL_SIGNUP_CTA`** (title, optional text, button label) — a CTA only, no form. It is restricted to the tenant whose slug is the platform owner's (`PLATFORM_OWNER_TENANT_SLUG`). The button opens the public page **`/ikaro-trial`**. Detail: `tenant-billing_MODULE.md`.
- `/ikaro-trial` (rendered with the Ikaro tenant's own branding, decision 71) shows feature descriptions (translated copy in the web app) and the **price table** (read from `FeaturePrice`, BR/US switch), then: Turnstile → Google sign-in (verified email and name) → form (business name, slug, country BR|US, **CPF or CNPJ for BR (decision 57)**, **Terms and Privacy checkbox**; language is derived from the country) → create tenant → **account-created / check-email page**. The signup endpoint lives in **Platform** and calls the existing `ProvisionTenantUseCase`; `TenantProvisioned` asynchronously creates the first manager and Billing trial. The visitor is not issued a tenant JWT or sent to the dashboard by signup; they use the existing first-manager invitation/login email once that work completes (CAND-22). The account-created page also offers a rate-limited "reenviar e-mail" and, once the first manager exists, a one-click Google sign-in to the existing staff login, since the visitor already holds a verified Google identity (both verified in story discovery).
- **US at signup and provisioning:** the country selector offers BR; **US is enabled by `FEATURE_FLAG_SIGNUP_US=true` once the Stripe adapter is live**, and the same flag blocks a non-exempt US tenant provisioned by script (§5.2), so no US trial can end without a way to pay. US prices exist in `feature_prices` from the start.
- Rules: slug 3–30 chars, `a-z0-9-`, no leading/trailing hyphen, reserved list generated from the **real top-level routes** (`dashboard`, `login`, `account`, `ikaro-trial`, `api`, `admin`, `www`, …) plus platform words (including `ikaro` and the owner slug), not changeable in v1 (extends the existing `Slug` VO, which today only checks the regex); **trial limit per Google account (below)**; Turnstile + per-IP rate limit; chatbot at tier `BASIC`; abuse and takedown: free-trial hotsites live on Ikaro's domain, so the reserved list also covers brand-impersonation words and the operator can suspend for abuse (`OPERATOR`, decision 87).
- **Trial limit.** A Google account may own several tenants. Its **first** self-signup tenant gets a free trial. A **further** self-signup gets a trial only while every earlier self-signup tenant of that Google `sub` has `has_ever_paid = true`; otherwise signup is refused with `SIGNUP_TRIAL_NOT_AVAILABLE` ("conclua o pagamento do seu teste atual"). Paying owners can add tenants freely; letting a trial lapse and signing up again is blocked until that tenant is paid or purged. The check reads `billing.signup_registrations (google_sub, tenant_id)`; Billing **reserves** the registration (`status RESERVED`) during the eligibility check under `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('signup:'||google_sub))` (no row exists yet, so a lock rather than a constraint), the `TenantProvisioned` handler completes it, and reservations not completed within one hour are released; the Platform signup endpoint reaches the check through BFF orchestration, not a cross-context port (decision 96); Gmail aliases and several Google accounts are accepted residual abuse, mitigated by Turnstile and the IP rate limit.
- Acceptance of Terms/Privacy (version + timestamp) is stored on the tenant's billing account.

## 7. Roles and visibility

Operator = Ikaro staff acting through scripts/internal endpoints (no backoffice screen in v1), used only for the price list, grace, suspension and the `exempt` switch (R12). There is no operator payment override or reactivation: provider-confirmed payment is the only way to restore access, **except by making the tenant `exempt` (R12/CAND-32) and except lifting an `OPERATOR` suspension with the audited `unsuspend` (decision 87)**; a break-glass reactivation was considered and declined (decision 97). The complete list of operator touchpoints is `tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md` §4. Plan changes are done by the manager (R11), and there is no per-tenant price negotiation. Every operator action is audited (who, when, why). Every MANAGER of a tenant sees its billing page; billing emails go to the billing email, falling back to the managers' emails. Alerts to Ikaro are **exception-only**, metric/log alert policies first (`tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md` §5); any email goes to the manager of Ikaro's own tenant through a separate non-billing ops template/path that the exempt skip does not apply to. There is no daily overdue/suspended list (decision 107).

## 8. Non-Goals / explicitly deferred

Annual billing and proration · coupons / free months · in-app payment-method forms · refund screens (provider console) · NFS-e issuing · FX conversion · currency or country change after start · operator backoffice screen · trial-extension action · additional limits beyond the chatbot tier (staff count, resources, services, lead submissions) · plan bundles / templates · usage-based pricing · replaying events produced during a suspension · consolidated invoice or an owner role for people with several tenants (every manager can cancel; a cancel email goes to the billing email) · self-service data export (later story G11) · trial extension (revisit if the data shows demand).

Hotsite publish state already exists (`hotsite_configs.is_published`, `<Unavailable />`, `manifest.isPublished` checked by the booking page, `FrontendRevalidationPort` for on-demand revalidation): suspension is a new *reason* for the existing state, not a new mechanism. `<Unavailable />` itself needs a change: it renders no `HotsiteAuthBar` today. Turnstile verification and the lead-form public-write pattern are reused. 72. *Stale charge and recovery charge:* when the sweep suspends for `NON_PAYMENT`/`PAYMENT_REVERSED` it cancels the stale open charge and issues the recovery charge at once; a `CANCELED` suspension issues it on "Reativar assinatura"; a stale link paid late reactivates like any payment. Operator-provisioned non-exempt BR tenants must be created with a valid `taxId` (`POST /internal/tenants` and the script), with a hold-and-alert safety net if one is somehow missing at issue time.
73. *Price notice:* TRIALING tenants are included in the price-change announcement (with ACTIVE and PAST_DUE), so no first invoice uses an unannounced price.
74. *Purge warnings:* two timestamp columns `purge_warning_30_sent_at`/`purge_warning_7_sent_at` on `billing.subscriptions` and a `TenantPurgeWarning` event written in the same transaction; reactivation clears both.
75. *Constants:* `CHARGE_ISSUE_LEAD_DAYS = 10`, `PRICE_CHANGE_MIN_NOTICE_DAYS = 30`, `RECOVERY_CHARGE_DUE_DAYS = 7`, grace = 14 days, defined once in §4.3.
76. *Chatbot tier names:* Básico / Plus (`BASIC`/`PLUS`) are final. `BASIC` keeps today's code defaults exactly (the `DEFAULT_X` caps and the current OpenRouter model `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash-0731`), so existing behaviour does not change.
77. *BR launch catalog:* HOTSITE R$ 49, BOOKING R$ 109 (recurrence and availability alerts included, decision 80), LOYALTY R$ 29, CHATBOT Basic R$ 39 / Plus R$ 79, LEAD_FORM R$ 19 (monthly, tax-inclusive) is approved as the seed for staging and the first launch; before launch any amount may change freely, afterwards an increase needs the announced notice (R4).
78. *US catalog:* US$ 12 / 26 / 7 / 9 (Basic) / 19 (Plus) / 5 is staging-only until Stripe's real fees and FX are confirmed (launch gates); slice E3 requires an approved USD catalog.
79. *Plus model:* the `PLUS` tier is evaluated with a **Kimi** model on OpenRouter instead of another DeepSeek variant (owner's call, 2026-10-27, for testing); the exact Kimi model id, the Plus caps and the price check are settled in story A1 from OpenRouter cost/latency data. The OpenRouter adapter traps documented for the chatbot (retry configuration, provider ignore list, `reasoning` effort handling, `provider.sort`, per-attempt timeout kept below the BFF timeout) must be re-validated for the Kimi model before it is offered; tier → model/caps lives in Platform code constants, never in Billing. Tiers are defined by caps and behaviour, not by model name, so the model can change without repricing.
80. *Features merged (owner, 2026-10-27):* `RECURRENCE` and `AVAILABILITY_ALERTS` are not separate features; they are part of `BOOKING`, whose price becomes R$ 109 / US$ 26 (US staging-only). `FeatureKey` has five members.
81. *Removing BOOKING:* rejected while any active booking, recurring schedule or alert extends on or after the effective date (`BILLING_FEATURE_IN_USE`); once saved, the bookable window is capped at that date. No cancel-only mode.
82. *Recovery charge periods:* a `RECOVERY` charge keeps `period_start`/`period_end` NULL; the period lives on the subscription and `paid_at` is its start. Charges are immutable apart from status and payment fields.
83. *Entitlement formula:* `SUSPENDED ? none : TRIALING ? all : active items`. Items are the selected plan only; no trial rows, no end-dating on suspension, no "restore" step.
84. *Trial end:* `trialEndsAt` is the tenant-local midnight closing day 14, like every other period boundary.
85. *Stale-charge race:* if the provider refuses to cancel a stale charge because it was paid, that charge is the payment (recovery cancelled); a double payment is refunded in the provider console and recorded.
86. *Settlement margin:* the sweep waits `PAYMENT_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_HOURS` (24) after a trial's end and re-reads the charge before suspending it.
87. *Operator suspension:* reason `OPERATOR` for abuse/takedown, no recovery charge, lifted only by the audited `unsuspend`; payment never lifts it.
88. *Cron helper:* explicit `includeSuspended` for data-maintenance crons (loyalty expiry, retention purges); feature-delivery crons exclude suspended tenants.
89. *Cancel timing:* `cancelAt` = end of the last paid period; a trial whose first invoice is paid cancels at trial end.
90. *Deferred add friction:* additions still start next period; add-feature conversion is reviewed after launch.
91. *Access cache:* asymmetric (operational 60 s/30 s, suspended 10 s) plus explicit invalidation on suspend/reactivate; the billing page bypasses the BFF cache.
92. *Timeliness:* the sweep runs hourly; open charges of at-risk tenants reconcile hourly; the payment-return page triggers an on-demand single-charge refresh (`charges.payment_return_seen_at` backs `paymentConfirmationPending`).
93. *Event gate:* outbound handlers must declare `skipWhenSuspended` explicitly (detector).
94. *Gating rollout:* the completeness detector starts in warn mode and becomes enforcing when the annotation story finishes; unannotated routes default to entitlement-allowed, suspension-denied.
95. *Production smoke test:* uses a dedicated non-exempt production test tenant, never Ikaro's own.
96. *Signup eligibility:* Billing reserves the registration under an advisory lock keyed by `google_sub`; the signup endpoint stays in Platform and reaches Billing through BFF orchestration.
97. *No break-glass reactivation (owner):* only provider-confirmed payment (or `exempt`, or `unsuspend` for `OPERATOR`) restores access.
98. *Alerts:* metric/log alert policies first, email secondary, exception-only.
99. *US/tax schema deferred:* the first schema is BR-only (`amount_minor` NOT NULL at insert, no tax lines table, no `tax_behavior`); slice E adds them by expand migration. The port keeps the tax shape.
100. *Billing email templates:* platform-level (tenant-less), not copied per tenant; **verified in code (2026-10-27):** `findAllByTriggerEvent(tenantId, …)` is tenant-strict and `tenant_id IS NULL` rows are only global defaults copied per tenant by `copyGlobalDefaultsForTenant`, so Billing emails need one small Notification change — a lookup for global (NULL-tenant) templates used by Billing send use cases — in story C6; no per-tenant backfill.
101. *Webhook verification:* for Asaas, a state-changing event re-reads the charge (`fetchChargeStatus`) and checks the amount before applying, so a leaked webhook token cannot forge a payment.
102. *New events and reminders:* `SubscriptionCancelled`, `PlanChanged` and `BillingReminderDue` (CAND-34) so cancellations and plan changes are emailed and audited, and the dunning cadence exists.
103. *Nota fiscal (BR):* go-live gate; see decision 109 for the zero-operator path.
104. *Success metrics and scale assumptions:* see §13.
105. *Milestone split:* the promotion sketch splits the work into milestones 1–3 plus later slices.
106. *Operator-free by default (owner, 2026-10-27):* R13 and `tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md`; every operator touchpoint is mapped; KPI = operator interventions per 100 tenants per month.
107. *No daily digest:* the daily overdue/suspended list for a human is removed; exception-only alerts replace it.
108. *Self-healing:* provisioning self-heal (creates a missing trial), provider health job (re-enables the webhook through the API if possible) and a **suspension freeze** while the provider is unhealthy (CAND-35), so outages never need an operator grace.
109. *NFS-e:* provider-automatic emission is configured once in the Asaas account and is a BR go-live gate; if the accountant confirms it needs the payer's address, the BR address becomes required before the first paid selection (like US). Manual per-charge invoicing is not acceptable at scale.
110. *Operator grace kept as a mapped edge case:* `grace_until` stays for provider-side failures the freeze did not cover (O9), never as routine work.
111. *Self-service receipts:* each paid charge in the history exposes the provider's receipt/invoice link, so "where is my receipt" never reaches support.

## 13. Success metrics, scale assumptions and operating model

- **Business metrics (read from billing events, no extra instrumentation):** trial → first payment conversion, add-feature conversion (decision 90), involuntary churn (suspended for `NON_PAYMENT` and not recovered), monthly recurring revenue by country.
- **Operating metrics (decisions 106–111, `tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md` §6):** operator interventions per 100 active tenants per month (target ≤ 1 after the first launch weeks), share of charges paid with no manual action, webhook lag, reconcile corrections per run, payment-to-reactivation time.
- **Scale assumption for design choices:** hundreds to low thousands of tenants in the first years. At that size the daily/hourly jobs, partial indexes and short-TTL caches are sufficient; nothing here needs sharding, queues beyond the existing Pub/Sub, or a separate billing service. If tenant count grows 10×, the first things to revisit are the per-tenant provider calls in the issue job (batching, rate limit 25,000 requests per 12 h on Asaas) and the access-port cache hit rate.
- **Operating model:** operator-free by default (R13). Operator work is exactly the edge-case list in `tenant-billing_OPERATIONS.md` §4; anything that shows up repeatedly there becomes an automation story, not a bigger runbook.
